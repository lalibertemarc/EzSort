# EzSort

## v1.0.1

- First release on CurseForge. No changes from v1.0.0.

## v1.0.0

- First release for WoW Forever.
- Sort button next to your open bags. Left-click sorts bags, shift-click sorts the bank while it's open, right-click flips the sort direction.
- `/ezs` sorts bags, `/ezs bank` sorts the bank, `/ezs reverse` flips the direction, `/ezs loot` flips whether new items fill your backpack or leftmost bag first.
- Key bindings for sorting bags, sorting the bank and flipping the direction (Options > Keybindings > AddOns > EzSort).
